## Belt

The alternator’s **pulley / rotation ring diameter is smaller** than what the original belt length assumed, so a longer belt was required: **45" V-belt** (not a shorter trial size—this is the length that works with the smaller effective pulley diameter).

## Wiring

- **Output:** **6 AWG** from alternator **B+** to the **starter solenoid B+ lug** — **unfused** (single cable to stud). Same junction as battery, breaker feed, etc.
- **Downstream of B+:** see **[fuse-box/b-plus-distribution.md](../fuse-box/b-plus-distribution.md)** (70 A breaker → Blue Sea **6 AWG**; MSD **10 AWG** + **10 A** inline).

No separate external voltage regulator — alternator self-contained per Powermaster application.

## Ground

Alternator case / ground strap to engine block per Powermaster instructions (see also [overview.md](../overview.md) grounds section).
